About Our School

Bongabon National High School (BNHS) is a fiscally autonomous secondary school nestled at the foot of the Sierra Madre mountain range in Brgy. Sinipit, Bongabon, Nueva Ecija. With a vibrant community of students and dedicated faculty, BNHS has long been a cornerstone of quality education in the region.

The school is equipped with 48 instructional classrooms including science laboratories, computer and internet laboratories, and a Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE) laboratory. Beyond academics, students have access to a library, clinic, multi-purpose hall, ALS room, rehearsal room, and school canteens — making BNHS a complete learning environment.

Recognized as the "Most Effective Secondary School in Region III", BNHS continues to serve as a pilot and model school, initiating transformative programs not just for students but for the broader community of Nueva Ecija.

Milestones & Achievements

2001 Adopted by SEAMEO INNOTECH (Southeast Asian Ministers of Education Organization Regional Center for Educational Innovation and Technology), establishing BNHS as a recognized center of innovation.
2003 Became a member of the SEAMEO Regional Coalition of Schools on Quality and Equity in Education (RCS-QEE), one of only five such schools in the Philippines.
2006 Formally introduced the Human Values-Based Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene Education (HVWSHE) project — piloting one of Southeast Asia's most impactful school-based environmental programs.
2007 BNHS science teachers co-authored a Science Activity Manual now used by over 40,000 students across the province of Nueva Ecija, embedding HVWSHE values throughout the curriculum.
